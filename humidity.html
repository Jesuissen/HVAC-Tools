Calculation basis & limits

Saturation vapour pressure uses Sonntag (1994). The default dew/frost-point input uses liquid water at 0°C and above and ice below 0°C; explicit over-water and over-ice inputs are also available. Relative humidity below 0°C defaults to liquid-water saturation pressure (WMO); ice reference can be selected in Advanced settings. No enhancement factor is applied. Dew/frost inversion is solved numerically. Recommended dew-point range is approximately -100°C to +100°C at atmospheric pressure. Wet-bulb temperature is solved from the ventilated psychrometer relation using γ = 0.000665 × P.
